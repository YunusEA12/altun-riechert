02Process

How the quick check works

Free and non-binding. Three steps, nothing more.

  1. 1

    You write to us

    Send us an email with your business name, location and, if you have one, your website. We need nothing else.

  2. 2

    You get your check

    We review your online presence and send you the results clearly laid out on one page.

  3. 3

    We talk for 15 minutes

    By phone or on site. Afterwards you decide whether and what we build.

What does the quick check cost?
Nothing. It is free and non-binding.
Do I have to buy anything afterwards?
No. After the call, you decide whether we build anything.
Why do you do this for free?
We are in our start-up phase and building our first references.
