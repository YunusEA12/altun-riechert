How the quick check works

  1. 01

    You write to us

    Email us the name of your business, the location and – if you have one – your website. That is all we need.

  2. 02

    You get your check

    We review your online presence and send you the results clearly laid out on a single page.

  3. 03

    We talk for 15 minutes

    By phone or at your premises. Afterwards you decide whether and what we implement at a fixed price.
